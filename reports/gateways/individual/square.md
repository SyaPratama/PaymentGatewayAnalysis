# Square — Deep-Dive Report

> Unified omnichannel commerce platform bridging physical POS hardware with web checkouts.

---

## 1. Overview

Square (Block, Inc.) is the market leader in bridging physical point-of-sale (POS) hardware with online e-commerce. It allows businesses to manage in-store physical payments, inventory, employee tracking, and e-commerce transactions within a single unified merchant dashboard.

---

## 2. Key Features

- **Omnichannel Unified Commerce** — Synchronized inventory, customer profiles, and ledger between offline POS and web.
- **Web Payments SDK** — Modern hosted fields for card entry, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Cash App Pay.
- **Hardware Integration** — Sleek countertop terminals, mobile readers, and Register hardware.
- **Terminal API** — Trigger in-store card reader charges directly from custom cloud software.

---

## 3. Fee Structure

- **Online Transactions**: 2.9% + $0.30
- **In-Person Card Swipes**: 2.6% + $0.10
- **Monthly Fee**: $0

---

## 4. Pros & Cons

### Pros
1. Seamless unification of physical brick-and-mortar stores with online e-commerce
2. High-quality developer SDKs and clear pricing structure
3. Flat-rate pricing with zero hidden merchant statement fees

### Cons
1. Available only in selected developed markets (no presence in Indonesia or Southeast Asia)
2. Higher fees for high-volume merchants compared to direct interchange-plus processors
3. Strict terms of service regarding restricted categories
