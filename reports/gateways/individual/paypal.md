# PayPal — Deep-Dive Report

> Global consumer payment brand and trusted cross-border checkout platform.

---

## 1. Overview

PayPal is one of the most recognized global consumer payment brands. While developers often criticize its historical API complexity, consumer trust remains massive. In many Western markets, offering a "Pay with PayPal" button significantly increases checkout conversion for consumers hesitant to enter credit cards on unfamiliar websites.

---

## 2. Key Features

- **PayPal Smart Payment Buttons** — Dynamically renders PayPal, Venmo, Pay in 4, and local options.
- **Buyer & Seller Protection** — Built-in dispute arbitration preventing unauthorized chargebacks.
- **PayPal Complete Payments** — Unified credit card processing alongside the PayPal wallet.
- **Global Brand Trust** — 400M+ active consumer accounts globally.

---

## 3. Fee Structure

- **Standard Checkout**: 2.99% + $0.49
- **International Transactions**: +1.50% cross-border fee
- **Monthly Fee**: $0

---

## 4. Pros & Cons

### Pros
1. Boosts conversion rates for international cross-border consumer e-commerce
2. Ubiquitous consumer adoption across North America, Europe, and Australia
3. No merchant setup or recurring monthly fee

### Cons
1. Higher transaction fees than credit card interchange or local aggregators
2. Opaque risk algorithms that frequently place 21-day holding reserves on merchant funds
3. Fragmented documentation spanning legacy APIs (v1) and modern APIs (v2)
