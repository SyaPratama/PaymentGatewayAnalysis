# Mollie — Deep-Dive Report

> The developer-friendly European payment gateway with transparent pricing.

---

## 1. Overview

Mollie is Europe's favorite developer-friendly payment processor. Founded on the principle of making payment integration effortless for European businesses, Mollie offers transparent pricing, drop-in hosted components, and native support for all European local payment methods without complex paperwork.

---

## 2. Key Features

- **Mollie Components** — Secure iframe fields matching your brand design.
- **European Local Rails** — Seamless support for iDEAL, Bancontact, SOFORT, EPS, Cartes Bancaires, and Klarna.
- **Recurring Payments API** — Subscription engine supporting SEPA Direct Debit and recurring card charges.
- **Intuitive Merchant Dashboard** — Clean, human-friendly interface for monitoring refunds and settlements.

---

## 3. Fee Structure

- **iDEAL**: €0.29 fixed
- **European Consumer Cards**: 1.8% + €0.25
- **Non-European Cards**: 2.8% + €0.25
- **Monthly Fee**: €0

---

## 4. Pros & Cons

### Pros
1. Superb developer experience tailored specifically for European commerce
2. Extremely transparent pricing with zero fixed contracts or monthly subscriptions
3. Very high consumer trust in the Netherlands, Belgium, and Germany

### Cons
1. Available exclusively to businesses registered within the European Economic Area / UK
2. Not available for Indonesian domestic acquiring
3. Standard flat pricing can be higher than Adyen at large enterprise scale
