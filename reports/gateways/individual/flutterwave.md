# Flutterwave — Deep-Dive Report

> Pan-African payment infrastructure connecting Africa to the global digital economy.

---

## 1. Overview

Flutterwave is a premier African fintech unicorn providing enterprise payment infrastructure connecting Africa to the global digital economy. With connectivity across more than 30 African countries, Flutterwave enables international corporations (including Uber, Microsoft, and Wise) to accept local cards, bank accounts, and mobile wallets across Africa.

---

## 2. Key Features

- **Vast Continental Footprint** — Accept payments across 34 African nations in 150+ currencies.
- **Mobile Money Aggregation** — Comprehensive coverage of MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Vodafone, and M-Pesa.
- **Barter by Flutterwave** — Consumer virtual card and cross-border remittance ecosystem.
- **Flutterwave Store** — Turnkey e-commerce storefront for micro-merchants.

---

## 3. Fee Structure

- **Local African Transactions**: ~1.4%
- **International Transactions**: 3.8%
- **Monthly Fee**: $0

---

## 4. Pros & Cons

### Pros
1. Broadest geographical coverage across the African continent
2. Robust multi-currency wallet architecture supporting USD, EUR, GBP, and local African currencies
3. Strong partnerships with global enterprise processors

### Cons
1. Past regulatory scrutiny and compliance audits in select African jurisdictions
2. Customer support response times can fluctuate during operational incidents
3. Occasional webhook delivery delays under peak transaction volume
