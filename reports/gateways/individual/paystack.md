# Paystack — Deep-Dive Report

> The gold-standard payments infrastructure of Africa (a Stripe subsidiary).

---

## 1. Overview

Paystack is the leading technology company powering digital payments across Africa. Acquired by Stripe in 2020 for over $200M, Paystack brings Stripe's world-class API standards, documentation, and reliability to the unique financial landscape of Africa, integrating domestic cards, bank accounts, mobile money, and USSD codes.

---

## 2. Key Features

- **Stripe-Standard Developer Experience** — Modern REST APIs, webhooks, and drop-in JavaScript popup checkout.
- **Pan-African Payment Rails** — Domestic bank transfers, USSD prompts, QR codes, and Mobile Money (M-Pesa).
- **Paystack Commerce** — Free digital storefronts and payment page generators.
- **Automated Split Payments** — Real-time revenue sharing for marketplaces and agency partners.

---

## 3. Fee Structure

- **Local Transactions (Nigeria)**: 1.5% + ₦100 (waived under ₦2,500; capped at ₦2,000)
- **International Cards**: 3.9% + ₦100
- **Monthly Fee**: $0

---

## 4. Pros & Cons

### Pros
1. The absolute gold standard of payment infrastructure across sub-Saharan Africa
2. Stripe-level developer ergonomics, reliability, and security
3. Low transaction fee structure with fee capping for domestic payments

### Cons
1. Geographically restricted to African business entities
2. International currency settlements require specific regulatory approvals
3. Limited relevance for domestic Southeast Asian e-commerce
