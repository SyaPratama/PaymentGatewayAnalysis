# Razorpay — Deep-Dive Report

> The market-leading payment and neo-banking platform of India.

---

## 1. Overview

Razorpay is the undisputed payments market leader in India. Processing billions in volume, Razorpay unlocked digital commerce in India through native support for UPI (Unified Payments Interface), 50+ netbanking portals, mobile wallets, and international cards with automated Indian regulatory compliance (RBI tokenization mandates).

---

## 2. Key Features

- **UPI Autopay & Collect** — Deep UPI integration covering Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm.
- **Standard Checkout UI** — Responsive drop-in modal checkout with one-click OTP verification.
- **Razorpay Route** — Split payment routing for marketplace vendors and commission deduction.
- **RazorpayX** — Neo-banking current accounts, automated vendor payouts, and payroll.

---

## 3. Fee Structure

- **Indian Cards, Netbanking, UPI**: 2.0% + GST
- **International Cards**: 3.0% + GST
- **Setup & Monthly Fee**: $0

---

## 4. Pros & Cons

### Pros
1. Best-in-class developer documentation and SDKs in the Indian subcontinent
2. Flawless UPI payment success rates and QR-code checkout flows
3. Full compliance with complex Reserve Bank of India (RBI) e-mandate directives

### Cons
1. Merchant onboarding requires a registered Indian business entity (or Malaysian via Curlec)
2. Not directly applicable for domestic Indonesian transactions
3. Additional documentation required to unlock international credit card processing
